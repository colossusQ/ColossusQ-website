Privacy Policy

How Colossus Q collects and uses the information you share with us.

When you contact Colossus Q, request a project quote, or share an event brief via our website form, direct email, phone, or WhatsApp, we collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

• Identity & Contact: Full name, corporate email address, contact telephone number, and job title.

• Organisation Details: Company or brand name, industry sector, and registered business address.

• Project & Brief Specifications: Event nature, expected attendee capacity, tentative dates, location requirements, and preliminary budget parameters.

We process your enquiry information solely to deliver high-calibre experiential production and event services:

• To review your requirements, prepare customized proposals, structural estimates, and creative presentations.

• To communicate directly regarding schedules, venue recce, permits, technical fabrication, and vendor coordination.

• To fulfil contractual and billing obligations with your company or agency partner.

Your data is stored within secured cloud infrastructure featuring enterprise-grade encryption (in transit via SSL/TLS and at rest). Access to project briefs and client contact records is restricted exclusively to essential Colossus Q management and production team members on a strictly confidential need-to-know basis.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your information. We only share necessary operational details with verified partners directly involved in executing your project:

• Production Ecosystem: Venue authorities, technical staging contractors, and fabrication crews (only necessary logistical details, never personal contact info without consent).

• Digital Infrastructure: Our secure CRM platform (Privyr), cloud communication providers (Google Workspace), and verified messaging gateways (WhatsApp Cloud).

We retain client records and event documentation for as long as needed to fulfill contractual obligations, provide ongoing post-event support, and comply with standard statutory financial accounting requirements under Indian law.

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections or updates, or ask us to delete your contact records from our communication databases at any time by emailing us at events@colossusq.com.

We use essential cookies and privacy-respecting aggregate analytics solely to monitor site performance, navigation speeds, and page traffic trends across different devices. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies or sell behavioural ad profiles.
