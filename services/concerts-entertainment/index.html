About concert and festival production in Hyderabad

Colossus Q is a concert and festival production company in Hyderabad. We produce live concerts, music festivals, artist performances, DJ nights, college festivals and ticketed shows, from stage design and structures to barricading, LED, sound and lighting. We have organised and produced Rangtaali for five seasons, and every show starts the same way: understand the crowd, then build the stage around them. We work with promoters, festival organisers and artist teams across South India.

Do you work with promoters and artist teams?

Yes. We produce shows for promoters, festival organisers and artist teams, and we organise our own, such as Rangtaali.

Can you handle crowd planning and barricading?

Yes. Barricading is in-house, and crowd and security coordination is part of our management support.

Do you supply LED, sound and lighting?

Yes, through trusted technical partners that we manage on site.
