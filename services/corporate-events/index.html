About corporate event production in Hyderabad

Colossus Q produces corporate events in Hyderabad and across South India: annual days, conferences, business summits, award ceremonies, town halls, dealer meets and MICE programmes. We design the format and stage around what leadership wants the room to take away, then build and run it with our own production team. Staging, structures and truss are in-house, with LED, lighting and sound from technical partners we manage. Venue, hospitality and logistics support can be added when you need it.

Can you handle the venue and hospitality too?

Yes. Event management is available as an add-on when you need it.

Do you work outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We deliver across South India.

Can you produce an event our team is already planning?

Yes. We can take on production alone and work alongside your internal team.
