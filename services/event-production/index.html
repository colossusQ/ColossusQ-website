About event production in Hyderabad

Colossus Q is an event production company in Hyderabad. We own our tent house, German hangars, stages, platforms, barricading and truss, and work with trusted technical partners for LED walls, lighting and sound. That lets us design, build and run production for concerts, festivals, corporate events, activations and exhibitions as one team. We produce our own events, such as Rangtaali, and provide production for brands, organisers and agencies, including white-label delivery.

Do you own your equipment?

We own our tent house, German hangars, staging, platforms, barricading and truss. LED, lighting and sound come from trusted technical partners we manage.

Can agencies hire you for production only?

Yes, including white-label delivery under your name.

Do you produce events for other organisers?

Yes. We produce for brands, organisers and agencies, as well as our own events.
