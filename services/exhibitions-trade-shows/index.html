About exhibition stall design and fabrication in Hyderabad

Colossus Q designs and builds exhibition stalls and trade show environments in Hyderabad. We handle booth design, custom fabrication, brand pavilions, product demonstration zones and exhibition technical infrastructure, then install and dismantle on schedule. Every space is planned around the people walking the aisle and what will make them stop. We work with brands exhibiting at trade shows and with exhibition organisers across South India.

Do you design and fabricate?

Yes. We design, fabricate, install and dismantle.

Can you build to the organiser's rules?

Yes. We work to the venue's and organiser's specifications.

Do you build pavilions as well as booths?

Yes, from a single booth to a full brand pavilion.
