About cultural festivals and public events in Hyderabad

Colossus Q organises and produces public and cultural events in Hyderabad and South India: cultural festivals, public celebrations, community and institutional events, and city-scale experiences. Our own tent house, German hangars, staging and barricading let us build festival grounds at scale. Our management support covers permissions, security and crowd coordination when needed. Rangtaali, which we have organised for five seasons, drew 50,000+ attendees over 10 days in Season 5.

Have you run large public festivals?

Yes. We have organised Rangtaali for five seasons. Season 5 ran for 10 days with 50,000+ attendees.

Can you manage permissions?

Yes. Permissions coordination is part of our event management support.

Do you work with institutions and public bodies?

Yes. Share the brief and the approval process, and we will plan around both.
